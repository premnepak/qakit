v1.0.4

Welcome to QAKit

Your all-in-one browser toolkit for QA engineers and developers. Here's everything that's new and improved since the last verified release.

What's new in 1.0.4
✏️
Screenshot Markup Editor Every screenshot now includes a ✎ Markup button. Annotate with pen, arrows, rectangles, highlights, blur/redact, numbered step markers, and text — then save or copy to clipboard.
🎯
Framework-ready Test Locators The Element Inspector generates Playwright, Cypress, CSS, and XPath locators at a click. Copy a ready-to-paste line for your test framework — no manual rewriting needed.
📏
Ruler — stack multiple measurements Click once to draw, click again to lock it as a permanent on-screen line. Accumulate several measurements side-by-side without losing previous ones. Press Esc to clear.
⌨️
Keyboard shortcuts work out of the box Region Screenshot (Ctrl+Shift+Z) and Full Page Screenshot (Ctrl+Shift+F) are pre-configured — no manual setup required after install.
💾
Screenshot Save always asks where to save The Save button in screenshot toasts now always opens your OS file picker, regardless of Chrome's download settings.
✅
Fully tested & verified release Every feature has been retested end-to-end before this release, backed by a complete automated regression suite to keep future updates reliable.

📥 One permission needed for screenshots

QAKit needs permission to save screenshots to your computer. Nothing is uploaded or shared — files go only to your local machine.
